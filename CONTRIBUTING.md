## Branch naming-
<p>Branch names corresponded roughly to the step or procedure we were 
adjusting at the time. </p>

## Commit Style - 
<p>Everyone seemed to employ unique, yet all minimalist commit formats.</p>

## Pulling / Pushing - 
<p>You should pull before pushing. While technical merging permissions will 
be handled separately by each repo, it is typically leads or higher-level 
personnel that maintain sovereignty over final merging.</p>

## Golden Rule - 
<p>Inspecting the codebase before going to merge, and of course even more
importantly is thorough communication. </p>
